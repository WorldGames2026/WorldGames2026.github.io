2. Data we collect ourselves

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal data on our own servers. Your game progress and settings (level, sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and financed by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To show and measure ads, Google may process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can be used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

Color Glow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Bavaria: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht). For requests concerning ad data, please also contact Google directly. For anything else, write to storegaori@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.
